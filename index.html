What the number means

Version 2.4 calibrates the market spread from 2022-2024 games, updates a team's state only after completed games, accounts for rest, and uses continuity, official preparation disruption, line movement, and sourced travel acclimation as capped confidence shifts. The scorecard above is the only evidence about this season: forecasts frozen at kickoff, graded against the market. Season ranges come from 5,000 simulations with persistent team-level uncertainty. Use the controls for late news the displayed inputs have not absorbed.

Sources: ESPN public score and injury feeds, Open-Meteo weather, nflverse historical schedules and injury reports, and saved preseason win totals. This is an analysis tool, not betting advice.
